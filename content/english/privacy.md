## SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

**What personal information do we process?** When you use our Services, we do not require you to create an account or provide personal information. All data you enter into GiftMemories (gifts, people, occasions, budgets, photos, and notes) is stored on your device and, if you choose, in your personal iCloud account. We do not have access to this data.

**Do we process any sensitive personal information?** We do not process sensitive personal information.

**Do we collect any information from third parties?** We do not collect any information from third parties.

**How do we process your information?** We collect pseudonymous usage analytics and crash reports through Firebase (operated by Google) to improve the app. This data is not linked to your identity.

**What are your rights?** Depending on where you are located geographically, the applicable privacy law may mean you have certain rights regarding your personal information. All of your data is under your control on your device.

**How do you exercise your rights?** The easiest way to exercise your rights is by contacting us at privacy@astrasoma.com, or by visiting astrasoma.com/support.

## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

### Information stored on your device

All of your data — gifts, people, occasions, budgets, photos, and notes — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device database (SwiftData). If you enable iCloud sync, a copy is also stored in your personal iCloud account. The data never leaves your device unless it is for your personal iCloud sync.

**iCloud Sync.** GiftMemories offers optional iCloud sync. When enabled, your data is stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service with a private database. This means:

- Apple encrypts your data in transit and at rest; Astra Soma LLC cannot access it
- You can disable iCloud sync at any time in iPhone Settings

For more information about how Apple handles iCloud data, see [Apple's iCloud security overview](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102651).

### Contacts

GiftMemories can import people from your iPhone's Contacts app, with your permission. When you grant access, the app reads:

- First and last name
- Birthday
- Phone number
- Email address
- Thumbnail photo

This contact information is stored only on your device (and in your iCloud if sync is enabled). It is never sent to Astra Soma LLC, Firebase, or any other third party. You can also add people manually without granting Contacts access.

You can revoke Contacts permission at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Contacts.

### Information we do NOT collect

GiftMemories does not require an account, email address, or sign-up of any kind. We do not collect, store, or have access to:

- Your name, email address, or phone number
- The names, emails, or phone numbers of people in your gift lists
- Gift names, prices, photos, or notes
- Your precise location
- Any information that identifies you personally

We do not sell, share, or rent personal information to anyone.

### Information automatically collected

We automatically collect certain information when you use the Services. This information does not reveal your identity, such as your name or contact details (it is pseudonymous), but may include device and usage information, such as your device model, operating system version, app version, information about how and when you use the app, and other technical data. This information is primarily needed to maintain the security and operation of our app, for troubleshooting, and for our internal analytics and reporting purposes.

The information we collect includes:

- **Log and Usage Data.** Usage data collected through Firebase Analytics, including which features are used and how often (for example, how many gifts are created, which tabs are viewed), your currency setting, and event counts. This data is pseudonymous and is not linked to your identity. Firebase Analytics does **not** collect gift names, person names, prices, photos, email addresses, phone numbers, or any content you enter into the app.
- **Device Data.** Device information such as your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and application identification numbers. Firebase may derive an approximate region from your IP address, but we do not collect precise location or GPS data, phone number, or mobile carrier information.
- **Crash Data.** When the app encounters an error, Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports including stack traces, device model, operating system version, and app state at the time of the crash. Crash data does not include any content you entered into the app.

### Payment data

GiftMemories offers optional subscriptions through Apple's App Store. All purchases are processed by Apple using StoreKit. We do not collect or store your payment information, credit card number, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Notifications

We may request to send you notifications regarding occasion reminders and other features of the app. If you wish to opt out, you may turn them off in your device's settings.

## 10. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Most mobile operating systems include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online.

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognizing or honoring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.
